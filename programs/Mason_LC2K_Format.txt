Labels <= 6 characters
Labels start with a letter, and only contain letters and numbers
Global labels like function addresses, Stack, and non-static data start with a capital.
All other labels start with a lowercase letter.

Opcodes and directives sit at the 3rd tabstop (i.e. after only 2 tabs).
Exactly a space separates operands in an instruction.
Comments sit at the 7th tabstop.
No trailing whitespace except 1 newline at the end of the file.

In the data section, function pointers come first.
Then single outputs/inputs. These are clearly marked in comments.
Then constants. First positive in increasing order, then negative in decreasing order.
Then other single data.
Then arrays.
Then the stack.
